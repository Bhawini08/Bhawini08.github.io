Research lens

Growth supportive, inflation easing

The framework identifies the state using lagged growth, inflation, rates, and volatility information before portfolio formation. It then conditions return and covariance estimates on observations from comparable states.

01 · Information SetIndustrial Production · CPI · 10Y Yield · VIX

Macro and market variables are lagged before portfolio formation to reduce look-ahead bias.

02 · EstimationRegime-conditioned moments

Sparse state estimates are shrunk toward expanding long-run estimates rather than treated as perfectly known.

03 · Portfolio DecisionConstrained long-only allocation

The optimizer incorporates portfolio constraints, turnover penalties, and transaction-cost assumptions before weights are accepted.

04 · ValidationChronological walk-forward test

Performance is compared with equal-weight, static minimum-variance, and risk-parity benchmarks using only information available at each rebalance.

About

A little beyond the numbers.

My background in economics and mathematics, together with early experience across venture capital and investor relations, drew me toward understanding how markets, businesses, and capital interact. Quantitative finance gave me a way to examine those questions with greater structure and rigor.

Today, I use quantitative methods to answer investment and portfolio questions, combining fundamental research, portfolio analysis, and systematic modeling. I am particularly drawn to problems where data requires interpretation, models require judgment, and decisions have to be made under uncertainty.

Outside finance, you'll usually find me exploring the city, experimenting in the kitchen, or buried in a book that has absolutely nothing to do with markets.
